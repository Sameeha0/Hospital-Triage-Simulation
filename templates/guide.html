Hospital Triage Simulation

Balance care, resources and public trust during an outbreak.
Game Guide Analytics

How to play

You are the triage officer during an infectious disease outbreak. Patients arrive one at a time, and for each one you choose to Admit, Isolate or Discharge.

What you see

  • Age: 1 to 90 years
  • Symptoms: visible symptoms
  • Exposure: recent exposure history
  • Comorbidity: chronic conditions
  • Risk: Low, Medium or High

Your decisions

  • Admit: treat in hospital, uses a bed
  • Isolate: isolate and treat, protects staff
  • Discharge: send home, best for low risk

Dashboard metrics

  • Day counter and available beds
  • Patients treated and recovered
  • Deaths and infected staff
  • Public trust (%)

End conditions

The game ends when any of these happens:

  • No beds are left
  • Public trust reaches 0%
  • Day 15 is reached

Strategy tips

  • Balance bed use against trust
  • Isolate high-risk patients
  • Watch staff infections
  • Track trust over time

Design notes

A self-contained Flask backend with a vanilla JavaScript frontend. The scoring is deterministic enough to teach trade-offs while staying engaging.
